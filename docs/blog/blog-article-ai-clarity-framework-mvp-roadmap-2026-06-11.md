## Draft

Many people have a strong web app idea, but no clear way to start. The problem is not always motivation. It is the gap between vision and execution: what should be built first, which tools are needed, what can wait and how a non-technical person can keep moving after the first prototype.

That is why I use the AI Clarity Framework. It is a practical process for turning an idea into an MVP roadmap through eight steps: Idea, Scope, Design, Stack, Workflow, Roadmap, Build and Autonomy.

Chaography is a good example. The project was well researched: a second brain around survivalism in an urban context. The thinking existed, the purpose existed, but the technical side had not started. With the framework, the first step was not coding. It was clarifying the real goal of the project, choosing the right tools, explaining the workflow to the client and defining a roadmap that could be executed in phases.

After that, we completed two phases together. The result was not only a small prototype. The client also had a workflow: how to organize the project, how to use the tools, how to continue without being a technical profile. This is the difference between delivery and autonomy. Giving someone a finished screen is useful for one day. Teaching them how the workflow works gives them leverage for the future.

AI changes what is possible for non-technical founders. With the right process, they can create a proof of concept, an MVP or even a beta version. But AI does not replace clarity. It needs a direction, a reduced scope and a roadmap.

Before building, ask: who is this for, what problem does it solve, what is the smallest useful version and what must the founder understand to continue?

## FAQ

**Who is the AI Clarity Framework for?**  
Solo founders, freelancers, creators and non-technical project owners who need a practical path from idea to first build phase.

**What should be cut from a first MVP?**  
Anything that does not prove the core value: advanced settings, secondary dashboards, complex automations and features made only because they are easy to generate.
