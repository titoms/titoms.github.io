## 9. LLM / GEO Optimization Notes

To be easier for LLMs and AI search systems to understand, each important page should include:

- clear page title
- direct answer in the intro
- structured sections
- FAQ
- definitions
- examples
- pricing when relevant
- author identity
- date published / updated
- internal links
- schema markup
- useful original insights
- project proof
- no vague marketing fluff

Use plain language and explicit phrases like:

- “This service is for…”
- “You will get…”
- “The process is…”
- “The price starts at…”
- “This is not for…”
- “The main outcome is…”
