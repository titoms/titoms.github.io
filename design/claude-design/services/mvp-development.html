IS IT A FIT?

Best results come from clear scope.

Good fit

  • You have a clear problem to solve
  • You can prioritize features
  • You have a realistic budget
  • You want a clean first version
  • You're open to a staged approach

Not a fit

  • A full marketplace/social network in two weeks
  • No budget
  • You refuse to reduce scope
  • You expect unlimited features
  • Equity-only work
